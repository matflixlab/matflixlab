about
A personal homelab running on a single-node k3s cluster hosted on an old laptop. Serves as a platform for learning Kubernetes, experimenting with self-hosted applications, and deploying real projects. All services are containerised, managed via Kustomize manifests, and exposed to the internet through a Cloudflare Tunnel — no open ports, no public IP required.
